What I do

Four services
01

Building a company second brain

I set up the knowledge system a business runs on: where documents live, how they are structured, and how AI tools read them. Without this, AI tools give generic answers. With it, they give answers based on how the company actually works.

02

Building agents and automations

On top of that knowledge system, I build automated workflows that take over repetitive parts of the business, from AI agents that handle routine tasks to automations that connect the tools a company already uses.

03

Training staff to use AI

One to one and in groups, mostly Claude and Notion. I teach people to build their own agents and skills, and I stay involved through the adoption period until it sticks.

04

Project and operations work

Scoping a problem, writing the plan, running it, reporting on it. This includes process design, hiring support, customer experience journeys and internal systems.
